## Needs legal review

- Halloween skit: don't use economic fear to create urgency or predict the economy.
- Affordability post: quote stats accurately with the source, and don't suggest a loan fixes affordability or homeownership.
- 30-day list: keep it separate from borrowing messages.
- Any post that mentions payments, rates or terms may need full cost disclosures (Truth in Lending, Reg Z).
- Paid partnerships need #ad disclosure under FTC rules.
